Why Bulgaria

Why set up in Bulgaria.

A member of the European Union with full access to the single market, Bulgaria offers one of the most competitive tax systems in Europe for entrepreneurs looking to grow their business.

Why set up your company in Bulgaria

10% Corporate income tax, the lowest rate in the EU

3–7 days Average time to set up the company
27 Member states accessible from Bulgaria

Legal form

OOD and EOOD

Equivalent to the French SARL and EURL, these two forms limit shareholders’ liability to the amount of their contributions and are 100% open to foreign shareholders, with no mandatory local partner.

Single shareholder

A single-shareholder limited liability company — for an entrepreneur who keeps full control of the company.

€2minimum share capital, paid up on incorporation
100%of the capital open to foreign shareholders

Multiple shareholders

A standard limited liability company — suited to a partnership with one or more partners.

€2minimum share capital, paid up on incorporation
100%of the capital open to foreign shareholders
